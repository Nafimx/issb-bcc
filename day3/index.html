Day 3 — Group Testing and DP's Interview

Planning on paper, then command on the ground, then judging the men you have worked with for two days. The remaining candidates attend the Deputy President's interview.

1 Planning Exercise (PE)

Candidates are presented with a problem scenario requiring collective planning and decision-making. The story is read out, you get a few minutes alone to write your plan, then the group produces one common plan and a nominated member explains it.

Scenario
10:00
  • List every problem first, then rank them: life, then limb, then property, then information.
  • Split the group by task, not by friendship. Name who does what.
  • Use only the resources in the story, and account for every one of them.
  • Give timings. A plan without a clock is a wish.
  • Solve all the problems, not the easiest two.

2 Current Affairs Discussion (CAD)

An interactive platform where candidates analyse and exchange views on recent national and international events.

Press for a theme.
15:00
  • Carry facts: dates, names, figures. One accurate figure outranks a paragraph of opinion.
  • Give the national angle — what it means for Bangladesh.
  • Read one national and one international daily every day until the board.
  • Disagree with the argument, never with the man.

3 Command Task (CT)

Candidates take turns leading a group through tasks to assess leadership and command abilities. You are called by name, you pick two helpers, and the obstacle is yours to solve.

Task
4:00
  • Repeat the brief back to the GTO in one line, then start — do not stand and think in silence.
  • Give orders by name: "Rafi, take the plank to the far drum."
  • Do the hardest part yourself; never send a helper where you would not go.
  • If the plan fails, say so, change it aloud and keep the task moving.
  • Choose helpers who work, not friends who agree.

4 Mutual Assessment

You evaluate every member of your group after the tasks — communication, teamwork, problem-solving and overall contribution. Fill your group's chest numbers below and rank them honestly; the board compares your judgement with its own.
