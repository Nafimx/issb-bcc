Day 1 — Screening (First Segment)

🧠

1 · Intelligence Test

Evaluates logical reasoning and problem-solving. 100 questions per batch, 24 fixed batches, five questions from each of the 20 screening categories.

Live
🖼️

2 · PPDT

View a hazy picture for a limited time, then write a story on your perception. 30s observe, 30s character, 30s action, 4 minutes story. 40 pictures.

Live

3 · Screening Result

Day 1 — Psychological Tests

🎞️

4 · TAT

Thematic Apperception Test — 11 pictures and a blank slide, 30 seconds to look and 4 minutes to write each story.

Live
⚡

5 · WAT

Word Association Test — spontaneous response to flashed words. 50 words per batch, weighted towards hard and military words.

Live
🧭

6 · SRT

Situation Reaction Test — 60 situations in 30 minutes, each with a model response to compare against afterwards.

Live
✍️

7 · SDT

Self-Description Test — five views of the same person: parents, teachers, friends, yourself, and who you want to become.

Live
