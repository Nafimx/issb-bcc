Day 4 — Final Assessment and Feedback

The assessors meet, some candidates are called again, results are announced and everyone gets a feedback session. Nothing you do today can undo three days of work — but the conference appearance and the way you take the feedback still matter.

1 Conference

All assessors convene to discuss each candidate's performance across all tests. The psychologist, the GTO and the interviewing officer put their independent assessments side by side; consistency across the three is what decides the case.

You are called in briefly. Walk in smartly, sit when told, answer plainly. Common questions: how you found the board, what you did well, where you fell short, and whether you would change anything.

What the conference is really asking about you
    • Do not argue your case or explain away a poor task. Own it in one sentence.
    • Do not run down another candidate.
    • Keep the same person you were on Day 1 — the file in front of them says who that was.

    2 Final Interview (if required)

    Some candidates may be called for a final interview to clarify any observations — a contradiction between the tests and the interview, a gap in the PIQ, or a borderline case. Being called is not a bad sign by itself.

    3 Result Announcement

    Candidates are informed of their selection status. Recommended candidates go on to the medical board; the rest are free to appear again if they remain eligible.

    • Take either result standing straight. The board watches this too.
    • If not recommended, ask for your feedback points and write them down the same day.
    • Eligibility for another attempt depends on the circular you applied under — check it before you plan again.

    4 Feedback Session & self-assessment

    Provides insights into performance and areas for improvement, regardless of the outcome. Do the same assessment on yourself first, honestly, then compare it with what you are told.
