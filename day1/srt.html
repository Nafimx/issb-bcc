Situation Reaction Test

Sixty situations, thirty minutes — about thirty seconds each. Each item is an incomplete sentence that you finish: most trail off on a lead-in such as “He…” or “so he…”, and a few are complete statements where you write the whole reaction. Write what you would do, not what you would feel. One line is enough: act first, then inform, then follow up. The clock runs for the whole set, so do not stall on one situation.
