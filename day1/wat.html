Word Association Test

One batch holds two sections: 50 English words and 50 Bangla words. Pick a section with the buttons below — run one, then the other, in whichever order you like. Each word is flashed on its own; give one short positive sentence before the next appears. Once a section starts the screen holds nothing but the clock and the word.

Make sentences

Do the drill first. Then press the button for one short model sentence per word of this batch — each is written to be finished inside ten seconds. Yours does not have to match; it only has to be positive, personal and practical.

At ISSB the words come about every fifteen seconds. Many are negative on purpose — blood, fear, defeat, enemy, war. The board watches whether your first instinct turns them into something constructive.
