Thematic Apperception Test

Four pictures, shown one by one. Each is on screen for 30 seconds, then you get 3 minutes to write a story on it. The test measures your power of observation, and because it is a psychological test the story must be constructive and positive.

Two stories are written in Bangla and two in English. Many candidates forget this and lose marks — the language for each picture is shown on screen while you write.
